# Sports pictograms — attribution

The nine sport illustrations in this folder (handball, athletics, archery, shooting, gymnastics, volleyball, wrestling, swimming and basketball) use adapted pictograms from **Pictogrammers Material Design Icons**, sourced from `Templarian/MaterialDesign` and distributed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.

Source project: https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign

Each SVG includes its individual original icon name in its leading comment. Changes consist of removing the library-specific SVG identifier and marking the icon as decorative. These are presentation-only pictograms; the separate historical departments card deliberately uses a year watermark.
